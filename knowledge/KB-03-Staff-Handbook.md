# Cedar Valley Clinic — Staff Handbook (Basics)

> ⚠️ FICTITIOUS document created for demo purposes only. Not real company data.

**Applies to:** All clinic staff · **Owner:** Practice Manager

## Paid Time Off (PTO)
- New full-time staff: **12 PTO days/year**, accrued monthly.
- After 3 years: **18 days/year**.
- PTO accrues from day one but cannot be used during the first **90 days**.

## Calling out / shift coverage
- If you're sick or can't make a shift, notify your supervisor at least **2 hours before shift start**.
- For absences of **3+ consecutive days**, a doctor's note is required.
- Find your own coverage when possible by posting in the staff scheduling channel.

## Required training
- All clinical and front-desk staff complete annual training: Privacy/Confidentiality, Safety, and Bloodborne Pathogens.
- New hires complete onboarding training in **week 1** before patient-facing work.

## Pay
- Pay is issued **bi-weekly**, every other Friday, by direct deposit.

## Systems
- **Patient Portal / EHR** — scheduling, records, messaging.
- **Knowledge Assistant** — ask policy & procedure questions in plain language.

## New patient checklist
- Arrive **15 minutes early** for your first visit to complete registration.
- Bring a **photo ID** and your **insurance card**.
- New patients under 18 must be accompanied by a **parent or legal guardian**.

## Intake forms
- Complete intake forms in advance through the **patient portal**, or on a tablet at check-in.
- Forms include: medical history, current medications, allergies, and emergency contact.

## Insurance & payment
- Insurance is **verified before your visit**. Bring an updated card if your plan changed.
- Co-pays are collected at check-in.
- Patients without insurance can ask about the **self-pay rate** and payment plans.

## Updating your information

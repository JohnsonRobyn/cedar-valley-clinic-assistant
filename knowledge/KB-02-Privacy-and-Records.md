# Cedar Valley Clinic — Patient Privacy & Medical Records Policy

> ⚠️ FICTITIOUS document created for demo purposes only. A simplified, privacy-style policy — not legal or medical advice.

**Effective date:** January 1, 2026

## Your privacy
- Your health information is **confidential**. It is shared only with your care team and only as needed for your care, payment, or operations — or as you authorize in writing.
- We share information with third parties (e.g., another provider, a family member) only with your **signed authorization form**.

## Requesting your records
1. Submit a **written records request** through the patient portal or front desk.
2. Records are provided within **10 business days**.
3. A small copying fee may apply for large or printed requests.

## Staff handling of patient information
- Staff access only the **minimum information necessary** to do their job.
- Do **not** discuss patient details in waiting rooms, hallways, or other public areas.
- Lock screens when away from your workstation.

## Privacy concerns or breaches
- A suspected privacy breach must be reported to the **Privacy Officer within 1 hour**.
- Patients with privacy concerns can contact the Privacy Officer directly.

# Cedar Valley Clinic — Appointment Scheduling & Cancellation Policy

> ⚠️ FICTITIOUS document created for demo purposes only. Not medical advice.

**Effective date:** January 1, 2026

## Booking an appointment
- Book by phone or through the **patient portal**.
- **New-patient visits** are 45 minutes; standard follow-ups are 20 minutes.
- Tell us the reason for your visit so we schedule enough time.

## Reminders
- An appointment reminder is sent **48 hours before** by text or email.
- Confirm or reschedule from the reminder link.

## Cancellations & no-shows
- Please give at least **24 hours' notice** to cancel or reschedule.
- A late cancellation (under 24 hours) or **no-show** may incur a **$35 fee** (fictitious).
- Three no-shows in a year may require a deposit to book future visits.

## Late arrivals
- If you arrive more than **15 minutes late**, we may need to reschedule so we don't delay other patients.

## Urgent needs
- For same-day concerns, call the front desk; we hold a few same-day slots each morning.
- **For a medical emergency, call 911** — do not wait for an appointment.
